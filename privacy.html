Privacy Policy — GearsOfWhy app

Last updated: 10 October 2026

What the app is

GearsOfWhy is a private tool used only by the owner of the Gears of Why YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload, schedule and edit videos on the owner's own channel;
  • read the owner's own channel and video statistics (YouTube Analytics);
  • read comments on the owner's own videos and post the owner's replies to them.

How the data is stored and shared

The sign-in token and the statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app does not collect data about viewers or other YouTube users beyond what YouTube shows on the owner's own videos.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also stops all access.

Google and YouTube terms

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
